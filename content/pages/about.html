I write here as बासभ (Basava). Kafkaintherun, kafka.blog, is a directory of random things: essays on science and history, poems, letters, short stories, and photographs, in Nepali and English.

I grew up in the Terai, reading at the top of a litchi tree because there was no electricity for a fan, and working my way through two cupboards of books that had once been an underground library. Muna Madan made me cry before I understood it; Parijat made me who I am. I have been writing ever since—on Twitter, on blogs, in notebooks—mostly to let out the voices that would not stay quiet.

By day, I work in immunotherapy and molecular biology research. Much of what I write about science comes from that bench: I want people to read about cancer, DNA, vaccines, and evolution in their own language, without jargon or rumors.

What you will find here

  • Science- cancer and the immune system, DNA, evolution, and the strange lives of eels, lobsters, salamanders and locusts. Usually in both Nepali and English.
  • History- long series on Sumer and Roman Britannia, and the stories of Nepal's own past.
  • Poems, letters and stories- मेरो कविता, mostly in Nepali.
  • Photographs- street photography, mostly from New York.

Every piece can be listened to as well as read: press Listen at the top of any post.

Why “Kafka in the run”?

Because of Gregor Samsa, and because of a line of Kafka's I keep coming back to: “A cage went in search of a bird.” And because most of this was written on the run- between experiments, on trains, at two in the morning- still in search of sleep, sanity, and the Shire.
