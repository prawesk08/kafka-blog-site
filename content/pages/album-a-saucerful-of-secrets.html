Why it matters

This is the album where Pink Floyd survived losing their leader and began to invent themselves as a band of textures instead of songs. The title track points straight to Meddle and Dark Side.

Tidbits

  • The only Pink Floyd album that features all five members, Barrett on just one track.
  • It is the first album with David Gilmour.
  • The long title track was built from drones, percussion and tape, and became a live centrepiece.

Why I like it

The mood is eerie rather than loud. It is the sound of a band finding a new way to exist, and I find that more moving than a polished record.
