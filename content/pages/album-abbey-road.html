Why it matters

The Beatles' farewell: warm, polished and quietly brave. Harrison's two songs here are among their finest.

Tidbits

  • It was the last album the Beatles recorded together, though Let It Be came out later.
  • The cover photo, with the four crossing the road, was taken on 8 August 1969.
  • Side two is a medley of short song fragments.

From my shelf
