Why it matters

The solo debut of the quiet Beatle, and proof he had been writing at their level all along.

Tidbits

  • A triple album: two discs of songs and a disc of jams.
  • "My Sweet Lord" was a worldwide hit.
  • Several songs had been turned down by the Beatles.

Why I like it

The spiritual searching is what draws me in. It is big and generous, like an open door.
