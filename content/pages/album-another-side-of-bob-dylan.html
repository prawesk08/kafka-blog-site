Why it matters

The moment Dylan stepped back from the protest role and wrote for himself. Folk purists complained; the poetry that followed owes everything to this turn.

Tidbits

  • It was recorded in a single evening, 9 June 1964.
  • It turns away from protest toward personal, surreal songwriting.
  • "It Ain't Me Babe", "My Back Pages" and "Chimes of Freedom" are here.

Why I like it

I like how it shows him changing his mind in public, quietly. "My Back Pages" is the best argument against being sure of yourself.
