Why it matters

Dylan at his most surreal and his most tender. It closes the electric trilogy that began with Bringing It All Back Home.

Tidbits

  • One of the first double albums in rock.
  • Most of it was recorded in Nashville with local session musicians.
  • "Sad-Eyed Lady of the Lowlands" fills an entire side.

From my shelf

  • Visions of Johanna — Dylan at his most mysterious. I hear something new every time.

Why I like it

"Visions of Johanna" is the song I never finish understanding. I hear something new every time.
