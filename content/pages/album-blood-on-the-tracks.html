Why it matters

Dylan's great album of loss and love, often called his best of the 1970s. It shows him turning the folk song into a novel.

Tidbits

  • Several songs were recorded twice, and the album mixes both sets of sessions.
  • "Tangled Up in Blue" tells its story out of order, and Dylan has changed the lyrics in live shows for decades.
  • "Shelter from the Storm" and "Idiot Wind" are also here.

From my shelf

  • Tangled Up in Blue — A whole novel in one song.

Why I like it

A whole novel in one song, and then another and another. I come back to it for the way it hurts without shouting.
