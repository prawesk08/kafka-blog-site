Why it matters

Folk-rock arrives. This is where Dylan plugged in and the poetry got stranger and faster.

Tidbits

  • The first side is electric, the second acoustic.
  • "Subterranean Homesick Blues" opens the album; its cue-card video is one of the first music videos.
  • "Mr. Tambourine Man" and "It's Alright, Ma (I'm Only Bleeding)" close it out.

From my shelf

  • Mr. Tambourine Man — Pure imagery: Dylan the poet.

Why I like it

It holds "Mr. Tambourine Man", the song where longing becomes melody, and "It's Alright, Ma", the one I turn to on a hard morning.
