Why it matters

The most hopeful of the Berlin albums, and its title track is an anthem.

Tidbits

  • The title track was recorded with microphones at different distances so Bowie's voice swells as he sings louder.
  • Robert Fripp played guitar on the title track.
  • Hansa Studios overlooked the Berlin Wall.

Why I like it

The title song is one of the greatest of all, about two lovers by a wall.
