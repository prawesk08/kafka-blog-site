Why it matters

The peak of Dylan's electric run. It proved that a rock song could carry as much language as a novel.

Tidbits

  • "Like a Rolling Stone", at around six minutes, was far longer than a single was meant to be.
  • Al Kooper, a guest, improvised the organ part that defines the song.
  • The album ends with the eleven-minute "Desolation Row".

Why I like it

"Like a Rolling Stone" is, for me, one of the greatest songs ever written: a spiral that strips you raw. "Tombstone Blues" is the other side of it, laughing at the world and daring you to join.
