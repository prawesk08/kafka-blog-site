Why it matters

The final studio statement of Led Zeppelin, softer and more melodic than before.

Tidbits

  • John Paul Jones's keyboards drive much of it.
  • It was their last studio album before Bonham's death in 1980.
  • It was released with several different outer sleeves.

Why I like it

It is an unexpected ending, gentler than the band's reputation.
