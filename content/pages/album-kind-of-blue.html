Why it matters

The cornerstone of modal jazz, and the record that opens jazz to people who thought they didn't like it.

Tidbits

  • It was recorded in two sessions, 2 March and 22 April 1959.
  • Davis brought only sketches of scales and melodies, and the band improvised.
  • It is often called the best-selling jazz album of all time.

Why I like it

It is the quietest, most patient music on my shelf, perfect for late nights.
