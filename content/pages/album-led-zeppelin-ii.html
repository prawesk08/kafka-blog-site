Why it matters

The heavy-rock blueprint. Its riffs defined what hard rock sounds like.

Tidbits

  • Engineer Eddie Kramer mixed it, and it reached number one in the US.
  • "Whole Lotta Love" features a famous theremin-like middle section by Page.
  • It was largely put together while the band toured.

Why I like it

I like how it never lets up: riff after riff after riff.
