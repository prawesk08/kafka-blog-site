Why it matters

Tidbits

  • Written partly in a remote Welsh cottage, Bron-Yr-Aur.
  • Half the album is acoustic.
  • "Immigrant Song" opens with Plant's wail.

Why I like it

The quiet side is what I love most. It shows how wide the band's range was.
