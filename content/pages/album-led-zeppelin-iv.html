Why it matters

The band's masterpiece and one of rock's best-selling albums.

Tidbits

  • The album has no title and no band name on the cover, only four symbols.
  • "When the Levee Breaks" gets its huge drum sound from a recording in a stairwell.
  • "Stairway to Heaven" is on this album.

Why I like it

It holds everything Zeppelin did well: heavy riffs, folk, mystery and one very long song.
