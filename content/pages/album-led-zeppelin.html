Why it matters

A blunt, thrilling debut that merged blues, folk and hard rock. It set the template for the hard-rock album.

Tidbits

  • Recorded in only a few weeks, mostly live in the studio.
  • The band paid for the sessions themselves and then signed with Atlantic.
  • The cover shows the Hindenburg disaster.

Why I like it

It sounds raw and hungry, like a band that knows exactly what it is.
