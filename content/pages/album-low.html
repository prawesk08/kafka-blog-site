Why it matters

A bold, fragmented album that changed the sound of rock.

Tidbits

  • Brian Eno collaborated on the synthesizer-heavy second side.
  • Visconti used an Eventide Harmonizer to give the drums their sound.
  • It is the first of the Berlin albums.

Why I like it

I love how abstract and cold it is, yet strangely warm.
