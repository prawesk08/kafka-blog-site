Why it matters

Meddle is where the post-Barrett band found its voice: patient, spacious and cinematic. Without "Echoes" there is no Dark Side of the Moon.

Tidbits

  • Side two is a single track, "Echoes", running over twenty minutes.
  • The ping that opens "Echoes" came from a piano note sent through a rotating Leslie speaker.
  • Opener "One of These Days" is an instrumental with Mason's one spoken line.

From my shelf

  • Echoes — Twenty-three minutes that never feel long.

Why I like it

It is the album I put on when I want a long, patient journey. "Echoes" never feels its length.
