Why it matters

A pop album with the ambition of a symphony, and an early landmark in the studio-as-instrument idea.

Tidbits

  • Lyrics are mostly by Tony Asher.
  • It inspired Paul McCartney as he worked on Sgt. Pepper.
  • "God Only Knows" is one of its best-loved songs.

Why I like it

It is sweet and sad at once, and the arrangements keep surprising me.
