Why it matters

A hard, defiant album made in difficult circumstances.

Tidbits

  • Recorded in a few weeks while Robert Plant was recovering from a car accident.
  • The album is almost all guitar, with little keyboard.
  • "Achilles Last Stand" is its epic.

Why I like it

I like its urgency. It sounds like a band with something to prove.
