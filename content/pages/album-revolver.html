Why it matters

Revolver is where the studio became the band's instrument. Psychedelia begins here, and it still sounds like the future.

Tidbits

  • Engineer Geoff Emerick and the band used tape loops and backward recording, especially on "Tomorrow Never Knows".
  • The cover is a drawing by the German bassist and artist Klaus Voormann.
  • It is the last album before the band stopped touring.
