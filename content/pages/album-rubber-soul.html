Why it matters

The Beatles' turn from pop to art. Folk, soul and Indian instruments appear, and the lyrics get personal. Everything after follows from here.

Tidbits

  • Recorded in about four weeks to be ready for Christmas.
  • George Harrison played the sitar on "Norwegian Wood (This Bird Has Flown)".
  • Folk, soul and Indian instruments appear for the first time on a Beatles record, and the lyrics turn personal.

From my shelf

  • Norwegian Wood (This Bird Has Flown) — The sitar that turned the West toward my part of the world.

Why I like it

I come to it for the sitar. The way the West began to turn toward my part of the world still feels special.
