Why it matters

A pop record made out of heartbreak, and one of the best-selling albums of all time.

Tidbits

  • It was recorded while two of the band's relationships were ending.
  • "Go Your Own Way", "Dreams" and "Don't Stop" all come from it.
  • It won the Grammy for Album of the Year.

Why I like it

It is polished and bitter at the same time, and I love that mix.
