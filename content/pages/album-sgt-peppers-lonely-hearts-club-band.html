Why it matters

The album that turned the LP into a work of art, and the soundtrack of the Summer of Love.

Tidbits

  • The cover is by Peter Blake and Jann Haworth.
  • For George Harrison's "Within You Without You", he worked with Indian musicians.
  • "A Day in the Life" ends on a huge orchestral crescendo and a piano chord left to ring.

From my shelf

  • Within You Without You — The Beatles reaching for Indian thought; it feels close to home.
  • A Day in the Life — Two songs stitched into a masterpiece.

Why I like it
