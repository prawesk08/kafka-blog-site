Why it matters

Bowie's first real statement, a mix of folk, psychedelia and space-age wonder.

Tidbits

  • The title song was released in July 1969, days before the Apollo 11 launch.
  • The album was first issued as David Bowie and retitled Space Oddity for its 1972 reissue.
  • It introduced Major Tom.

Why I like it

I like the early, searching Bowie, before the costumes.
