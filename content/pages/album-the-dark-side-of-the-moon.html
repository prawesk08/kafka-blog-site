Why it matters

A single, continuous suite about time, money, madness and conflict, with the studio used as an instrument. It showed that a rock album could be one long thought.

Tidbits

  • Alan Parsons engineered the sessions.
  • The prism cover is by Storm Thorgerson and Hipgnosis.
  • It spent hundreds of weeks on the US Billboard 200 and is among the best-selling albums ever made.

From my shelf

  • Time — The alarm clocks, and then the truth about wasted years.
  • Us and Them — War and division, sung softly.

Why I like it

It begins with a heartbeat and ends with one. "Time" still tells me the truth about wasted years, and "Us and Them" says what war is in a whisper.
