Why it matters

A debut that mixed blues, poetry and menace and made the Doors a legend.

Tidbits

  • The band had no bass player; Manzarek played bass lines on a keyboard.
  • "The End" runs over eleven minutes and later featured in Apocalypse Now.
  • "Light My Fire" was a hit single.

From my shelf

  • The End — Dark, long and hypnotic. Apocalypse Now made it unforgettable.
  • Light My Fire — That organ solo still feels like a fever.

Why I like it

"The End" is dark and hypnotic, and I can never listen to it without picturing Apocalypse Now.
