Why it matters

This is where Dylan becomes Dylan: a 21-year-old making protest songs that read like poems. It set what a singer-songwriter could say.

Tidbits

  • Most of the songs are his own, a big step from the folk standards of his debut.
  • "Blowin' in the Wind" and "A Hard Rain's A-Gonna Fall" both appear here.
  • The cover photograph, of Dylan walking with Suze Rotolo down a snowy Jones Street, became one of the most imitated sleeves in music.

From my shelf

  • Blowin' in the Wind — The question that still has no answer.

Why I like it

"Blowin' in the Wind" was one of the songs that stayed on my MP3 player for years. I still wonder how a 21-year-old could ask a question that large.
