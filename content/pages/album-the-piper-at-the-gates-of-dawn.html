Why it matters

Pink Floyd's debut is the sound of London's psychedelic underground at its brightest. It is also the only Floyd album led by Barrett, and his brief, brilliant run is part of why the band's later records are so haunted.

Tidbits

  • The title comes from a chapter of Kenneth Grahame's The Wind in the Willows.
  • Syd Barrett wrote most of the songs; he was gone from the band within a year.
  • "Astronomy Domine" and "Interstellar Overdrive" set the template for British space rock.

Why I like it

It is strange, playful and a little fragile, the band before the grand concepts. I keep it for the way it makes the English garden sound like outer space.
