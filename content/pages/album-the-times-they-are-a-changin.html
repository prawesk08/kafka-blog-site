Why it matters

The most openly political Dylan album, and the one that fixed his image as the voice of a generation, a label he spent the next decade running away from.

Tidbits

  • Every song on the album is an original.
  • It carries "Only a Pawn in Their Game" and "The Lonesome Death of Hattie Carroll".
  • The title song was written as a deliberate anthem.
