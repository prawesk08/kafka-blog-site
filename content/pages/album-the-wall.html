Why it matters

A double album that turned alienation into a full rock opera. It marks both Pink Floyd's biggest commercial peak and the point where the band began to come apart.

Tidbits

  • Conceived mainly by Roger Waters as a story of isolation, with a wall built brick by brick.
  • It was adapted into the 1982 film The Wall, directed by Alan Parker.
  • "Comfortably Numb" combined a Gilmour melody with Waters's verses.

From my shelf

  • Comfortably Numb — Gilmour's solo is where the song finally breaks open.

Why I like it

It is dark and theatrical, and "Comfortably Numb" is the moment the whole thing breaks open: Gilmour's solo is the best two minutes of the record.
