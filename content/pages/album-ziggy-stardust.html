Why it matters

The glam masterpiece. It made Bowie a star and turned rock into theatre.

Tidbits

  • The full title is The Rise and Fall of Ziggy Stardust and the Spiders from Mars.
  • "Starman" was performed on Top of the Pops in July 1972.
  • It is a concept album about a doomed alien rock star.

Why I like it

It is a story about fame told as a rock opera, and the last song, "Rock 'n' Roll Suicide", gets me every time.
