Why it matters

A novel that turns Nepali fiction inward, from society to the mind.

Tidbits

  • Parijat is the pen name of Bishnu Kumari Waiba, who was born in Darjeeling and wrote in Nepali.
  • It was published in 1964, in the years just before Shirishko Phool made her famous.

Why I like it

The Parijat I go back to when I want her quieter, turned inward instead of outward.
