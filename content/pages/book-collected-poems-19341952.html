Why it matters

Poems that rage, sing and refuse to behave.

Tidbits

  • Dylan Thomas was Welsh; the Collected Poems appeared in 1952.
  • Bob Dylan took his stage name from, or was thought to have taken it from, Dylan Thomas, though he has been vague about it.
  • The collection includes Do Not Go Gentle into That Good Night.

Lines worth keeping

“Do not go gentle into that good night.”

Why I like it

I once mixed him up with Bob Dylan. Now I read him for the rage.
