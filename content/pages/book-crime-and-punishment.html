Why it matters

A murder story in which the real trial happens inside the murderer.

Tidbits

  • It was serialised in The Russian Messenger in 1866.
  • The murderer is known to the reader from the start; the suspense is moral, not detective.
  • Raskolnikov's name comes from the Russian word for schism.

Lines worth keeping

“Pain and suffering are always inevitable for a large intelligence and a deep heart.”
