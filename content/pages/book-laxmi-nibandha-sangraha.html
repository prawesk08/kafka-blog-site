Why it matters

Devkota's prose shows the same overflowing mind as his verse.

Tidbits

  • Laxmi Prasad Devkota is known as the Mahakavi, the great poet of Nepali literature.
  • Devkota is best known for the verse epic Muna Madan, but he wrote widely in prose too.
  • This collection shows him as an essayist, not only a poet.

Why I like it

I first met Devkota through his poem पागल; these essays showed me he could laugh too.
