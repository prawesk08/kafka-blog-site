Why it matters

The book to open when I doubt whether I should keep writing.

Tidbits

  • The letters were written between 1903 and 1908 to Franz Xaver Kappus, a young cadet.
  • Kappus published them in 1929, after Rilke's death.
  • Rilke advises him to look inward and ask whether he must write.

Lines worth keeping

“Be patient toward all that is unsolved in your heart and try to love the questions themselves.”

Why I like it

The book I go back to whenever I doubt I should keep writing.
