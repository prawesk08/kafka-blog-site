Why it matters

Rimal broke the metre, and Nepali poetry changed with it.

Tidbits

  • Gopal Prasad Rimal is credited with bringing free verse into Nepali poetry.
  • Masan is a play and Aamako Sapana is a poem; both are landmarks of modern Nepali writing.

Why I like it

Rimal broke the metre, and that freedom is why Nepali poems can sound the way mine do.
