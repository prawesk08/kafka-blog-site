Why it matters

The novel that made Parijat a household name in Nepali literature.

Tidbits

  • It won the Madan Puraskar, one of Nepal's most important literary prizes.
  • It is Parijat's best-known novel, published in 1965 (2022 BS).
  • The story centres on a former soldier and a dying young woman.

Why I like it

The Parijat everyone starts with, and the one I keep coming back to: a soldier, a dying girl and a flower that does not mean what it seems.
