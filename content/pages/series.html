Shows I have watched and liked, and the history I can't stop watching.

Drama

Doordarshan

Malgudi Days

Created by Shankar Nag (from R. K. Narayan) · Master Manjunath · 1987

Childhood television; simple stories that still feel like home.

NBC

The West Wing

Created by Aaron Sorkin · Martin Sheen, Allison Janney · 1999–2006

“What's next?”

Politics as it should be. I still believe in Bartlet's White House.

HBO

The Wire

Created by David Simon · Dominic West, Sonja Sohn · 2002–2008

“All the pieces matter.”

A whole city as the main character. Nothing else comes close.

HBO

The Newsroom

Created by Aaron Sorkin · Jeff Daniels, Emily Mortimer · 2012–2014

“America is not the greatest country in the world anymore.”

Idealistic journalism, Sorkin speeches and all.

Created by Christopher Cantwell & Christopher C. Rogers · Lee Pace, Mackenzie Davis · 2014–2017

“Computers aren't the thing. They're the thing that gets us to the thing.”

“There's always money in the banana stand.”

The Office (US)

Created by Greg Daniels · Steve Carell, Jenna Fischer · 2005–2013

“That's what she said.”

Cringe comedy with a warm heart.

Schitt's Creek

Fleabag
