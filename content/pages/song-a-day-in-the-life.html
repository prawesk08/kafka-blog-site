Why it matters

It is the closing statement of Sgt. Pepper and the Beatles at their most ambitious.

Tidbits

  • The song stitches together two unfinished pieces, one by Lennon and one by McCartney, using a huge orchestral crescendo.
  • Part of it was prompted by newspaper stories Lennon read, including the death of Tara Browne.
  • The final piano chord was struck on several pianos at once and left to ring for around 40 seconds.

Why I like it

Two songs stitched into a masterpiece.
