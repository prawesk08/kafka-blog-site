Why it matters

A bright, bouncy tune carrying a warning that has only become more urgent.

Tidbits

  • Mitchell wrote it after looking out of a hotel window in Hawaii and seeing a car park beyond the mountains.
  • It is one of the earliest pop songs with a clearly environmental message.
  • It has been covered many times, including a hit version by Counting Crows with Vanessa Carlton in 2002.

Why I like it

A cheerful tune with a sharp warning.
