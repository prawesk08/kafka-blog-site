Why it matters

It turned protest into a question, which is why it has never gone out of date.

Tidbits

  • Dylan wrote it in 1962; Peter, Paul and Mary's cover reached No. 2 in the US in 1963, before his own album came out.
  • It became one of the defining songs of the American civil rights movement.
  • Sam Cooke is said to have been so struck by it that he went on to write "A Change Is Gonna Come".
