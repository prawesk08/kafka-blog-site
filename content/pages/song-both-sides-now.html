Why it matters

It is a song about learning that you have only ever seen one side of anything.

Tidbits

  • Mitchell began writing it in 1966 after reading a passage in Saul Bellow's Henderson the Rain King on a flight.
  • Judy Collins recorded it first and had a hit with it in 1967; Mitchell's own version came on Clouds in 1969.
  • She recorded it again with an orchestra in 2000, at a very different stage of life.
