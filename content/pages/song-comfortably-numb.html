Why it matters

It balances a muted verse against one of rock's biggest guitar solos.

Tidbits

  • Gilmour wrote the music and Waters the words; they disagreed over how it should be arranged.
  • Waters has said the lyrics came from being sedated to get through a 1977 show.
  • Gilmour's closing solo is often counted among the greatest ever recorded.

Why I like it

Gilmour's solo is where the song finally breaks open.
