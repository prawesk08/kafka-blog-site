Why it matters

It is the moment Pink Floyd found the long, patient shape that made them.

Tidbits

  • It fills the second side of Meddle at nearly 23 and a half minutes.
  • The opening 'ping' is a piano note run through a Leslie speaker.
  • The song grew out of band improvisations and studio experiments, and was filmed for Live at Pompeii in 1972.

Why I like it

Twenty-three minutes that never feel long.
