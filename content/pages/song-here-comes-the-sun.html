Why it matters

The most hopeful two minutes on Abbey Road, written by the quietest Beatle.

Tidbits

  • Harrison wrote it in Eric Clapton's garden after slipping away from a business meeting at Apple.
  • It uses a Moog synthesizer and shifting time signatures in the bridge.
  • It has become one of the most-streamed Beatles songs.

Why I like it

Harrison's quiet joy, the warmest song they made.
