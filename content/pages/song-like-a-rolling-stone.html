Why it matters

It broke the rules of what a single could be about and how long it could last.

Tidbits

  • The single ran over six minutes, which was unheard of for radio at the time.
  • Al Kooper came to the session as a guest, ended up at the organ and improvised the riff everyone remembers.
  • Rolling Stone placed it first on its 2004 list of the 500 greatest songs.
