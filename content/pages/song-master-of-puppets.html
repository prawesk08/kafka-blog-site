Why it matters

The record that made metal a serious, structured art form.

Tidbits

  • It is about addiction, told from the point of view of the drug.
  • It was Cliff Burton's last album with the band; he died in September 1986.
  • In 2015 it was added to the US National Recording Registry.

Why I like it

Eight minutes of riffs about control.
