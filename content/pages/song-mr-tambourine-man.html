Why it matters

It showed that a pop song could be built almost entirely from imagery, with no story at all.

Tidbits

  • The Byrds' electric cover went to No. 1 in the US and the UK in 1965 and helped launch folk rock.
  • Dylan's own recording is acoustic, opening the second side of the album's acoustic half.
  • The musician Bruce Langhorne, who played guitar on the session, is often said to have inspired the song's "tambourine man".
