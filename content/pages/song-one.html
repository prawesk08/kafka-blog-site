Why it matters

A war song that is also one of the heaviest anti-war statements in music.

Tidbits

  • It was the band's first music video, using scenes from the film Johnny Got His Gun.
  • The story is told by a soldier who has lost everything but his mind.
  • It brought Metallica its first Grammy.

Why I like it
