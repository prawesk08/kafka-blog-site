Why it matters

A long goodbye to a lost friend.

Tidbits

  • It is a tribute to Syd Barrett, the band's founder.
  • Barrett turned up, unannounced, at the studio during the sessions.
  • It opens with Gilmour's four-note guitar phrase, often called Syd's theme.

Why I like it

A tribute to a lost friend, and those four opening notes.
