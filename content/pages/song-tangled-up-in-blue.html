Why it matters

It treats time like a painter treats space, letting past and present sit side by side.

Tidbits

  • It opens Blood on the Tracks; the album version was re-recorded in Minneapolis after the New York sessions.
  • Dylan has credited lessons with the painter Norman Raeben for the way the song moves around in time.
  • He has rewritten the lyrics many times on stage, so no two live versions match.
