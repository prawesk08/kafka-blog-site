Why it matters

A folk song handed to rock and blues, and it never went back.

Tidbits

  • It is an old folk song; the Animals' arrangement is built on Alan Price's organ and Hilton Valentine's guitar.
  • It went to No. 1 in both the UK and the US in 1964.
  • The Animals' arrangement came after Dylan had recorded the song on his 1962 debut album.

Why I like it

That opening guitar, and you're gone.
