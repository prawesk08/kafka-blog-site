Why it matters

It gave a whole generation its anthem, and every generation since has borrowed it.

Tidbits

  • Dylan recorded it in October 1963 and made it the title track of his third album.
  • He has said he wanted to write a big song with short, direct verses, in the manner of the old Irish and Scottish ballads.
  • It is still used as shorthand for the generational upheaval of the sixties.
