Why it matters

For many fans this is the high point of Dylan's writing: dense, dreamlike and never quite explained.

Tidbits

  • Dylan first tried it in New York in late 1965 under a different title, then recorded the finished version in Nashville.
  • It appears on Blonde on Blonde, often described as the first double studio album in rock.
  • The Nashville session players were brought in to give it its hushed, late-night feel.
