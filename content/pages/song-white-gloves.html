Why it matters

The sound that introduced Khruangbin: soft, groove-heavy and instantly warm.

Tidbits

  • The band formed in Houston, and their name is the Thai word for aeroplane.
  • The three members are Laura Lee on bass, Mark Speer on guitar and Donald 'DJ' Johnson on drums.
  • The Universe Smiles Upon You was recorded in a barn in Burton, Texas.
