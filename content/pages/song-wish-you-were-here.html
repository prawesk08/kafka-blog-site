Why it matters

A plain song about absence.

Tidbits

  • It begins with a radio being tuned, then a 12-string acoustic guitar.
  • The violinist Stéphane Grappelli recorded a part in a nearby studio, though it sits very low in the mix.
  • It is the title track of the album.

Why I like it

The loneliest song I know.
