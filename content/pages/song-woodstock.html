Why it matters

The best song about Woodstock was written by someone who wasn't there.

Tidbits

  • Mitchell never played Woodstock; her manager advised her to do a television appearance instead, and she wrote the song from Graham Nash's account of the festival.
  • Crosby, Stills, Nash and Young turned it into a hit rock version in 1970.
  • Her own recording is slower and more mysterious, built on electric piano.

Why I like it

The hippie dream, sung by someone who missed it.
