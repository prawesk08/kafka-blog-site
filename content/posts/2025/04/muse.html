do you remember;
the morning and
the evening bells
when you were at school?
both sounded
the same - yet:

one would-
always makes you sad,
and the other-
always brought you joy,
even though both-
sounded the same,
they were not-
one and the same.

tomorrow is not-
same as our yesterday-
we are not -
all our yesterdays.

just because your -
last love hurt you
doesn't mean -
it always will.

'a fishermen knows-
the sea is dangerous
and the storm is terrible',
but that doesn't -
keep him ashore.

it's his hope:
of chasing dreams-
of loving the living-
and the fight of becoming,
that brings him back
into the open sea,
today, tomorrow
and all the days after.

haunt me beautiful-
haunt me beautifully,
bit by bit, then all at once?

share me-
your dreams;
all your little things,
and all your whims-
"you, be you"
warm me-
in your darkness,
wrap me with
your scars and-
all your nightmares.

the quiet pauses
between the words
and the silence
between the breaths.

In this Universe..
March 11, 2025.
